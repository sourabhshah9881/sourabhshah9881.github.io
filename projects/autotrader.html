AutoTrader / Algostra - Algo Trading Platform and Algorythm Storefront

A strategy creation, backtesting and live-trading platform, paired with a storefront for browsing and subscribing to research-analyst strategies.

Problem & role

Problem & users

Traders who build their own strategies need creation, intraday backtesting, live order/position tracking and P&L in one place. Traders who want someone else's strategy need to browse, subscribe to and deploy a research analyst's black-box strategy safely and compliantly.

My role

Own the Spring Boot backend and Vue 3 frontend for the main trading app, and the separate Vite storefront SPA, including Keycloak SSO, eSign onboarding and subscription management.

Architecture

Key engineering decisions

  • The main app frontend is served straight from the Spring Boot jar, keeping the trading app a single deployable artifact.
  • The storefront is a separate Vite SPA, decoupling its release cadence from the authenticated trading app it sits in front of.
  • Keycloak SSO plus eSign onboarding gate subscription and compliance steps before a user can deploy someone else's strategy.
  • Subscription management includes auto-renewal, avoiding manual resubscription friction for storefront strategies.
  • Both frontends are type-checked with Vitest coverage despite different build setups.
